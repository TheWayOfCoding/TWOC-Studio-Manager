PC link (audio wire)

Talk to the main PC over two headphone cables: cable A carries commands out, cable B carries confirmations back. Work top to bottom: assign the ports once, test the wire, then boot OBS.

1 Wire ports

TX plays out of the hub (green jack); RX listens on the hub (red jack). Selections persist on the hub.

2 Test the wire

A ping should come back confirmed within seconds; listen hears whatever is currently on the wire.

Send+Wait listens 8s (ping) / 60s (obs) for the ack

3 Boot the main PC's OBS

Pings until the PC answers, starts OBS, then cuts to the collection and scene. Takes minutes on a cold PC; the status line follows automatically until it finishes.

Full fixture range. Power past 10% needs the allow bright tick on its row — these lights are blinding, and that tick is the only warning you get. Step time is decimal seconds held per step: power steps whole percents, color steps fixture increments. Look fades step power first, then color.
