PC link (audio wire)

Talk to the main PC over two headphone cables: cable A carries commands out, cable B carries confirmations back. Work top to bottom: assign the ports once, test the wire, then boot OBS.

1. Wire ports. TX plays out of the hub (green jack); RX listens on the hub (red jack). Selections persist on the hub.
TX port (cable A out) RX port (cable B in)
2. Test the wire. A ping should come back confirmed within seconds; listen hears whatever is currently on the wire.
command Send+Wait listens 8s (ping) / 60s (obs) for the ack
3. Boot the main PC's OBS. Pings until the PC answers, starts OBS, then cuts to the collection and scene. Takes minutes on a cold PC; the status line follows automatically until it finishes.
